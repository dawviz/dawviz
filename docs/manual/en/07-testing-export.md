# Testing video export

[Contents](README.md) · [Tiếng Việt](../07-thu-nghiem.md)

This demo is being tried on many computers, because video encoding depends a lot on the computer and the browser. If you're willing, follow the steps below (about 10 minutes) and send us the results. You only need a GitHub account, and you don't have to send your music project.

## Step 1. Check this computer

1. Open the app and click **Try the sample project**.
2. Click **Export video**, then **Check this computer**. The app really encodes a few frames and one second of audio. It takes a few seconds.
3. Click **Copy result**. The result only has browser and computer information, no file names or project content.

## Step 2. Try an export

1. Keep the default settings (1080p, 30 fps), or pick 720p and 24 fps if your computer is slow. Click **Start export**.
2. Note which format the app chose, whether it finished, how long it took and the speed shown (for example 2×).
3. Open the exported file in a normal video player. Does it play, and does the picture look right?

## Step 3. Check sound against picture (if you have audio)

1. Load a short mix with a clear hit (for example a drum on the first beat), in the Audio section of the Preview tab.
2. Export the video.
3. Play it: listen for the first sound and watch the first note touch the playhead. Do they happen together? If not, is the sound early or late?

## Send the results

Click **Send the result** in the export dialog, or open the [result form](https://github.com/dawviz/dawviz/issues/new?template=test-result.yml), paste the Step 1 result and answer a few questions. For general feedback use the [feedback form](https://github.com/dawviz/dawviz/issues/new?template=feedback.yml).

Two kinds of computers are especially useful: Windows laptops (AAC sometimes reports as supported but fails when encoding) and phones or tablets (sound and picture may drift apart). Thank you!
